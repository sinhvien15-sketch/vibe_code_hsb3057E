About the School

HSB — School of Business & Management

Founded in 1995, HSB is a prestigious autonomous member school of Vietnam National University, Hanoi. Recognized for international standards, HSB delivers undergraduate and graduate education that shapes dynamic global leaders for the digital era.

Who We Are

Vision, Mission & Core Values

01 — Mission
Shape Tomorrow's Leaders

HSB educates young talents to become leaders, executives, and managers for both the Public and Private sectors — bridging academic rigor with real-world impact.

02 — Vision
Global Standard. Local Heart.

A reputable School of Business and Management recognized in Vietnam and globally — known for interdisciplinary education integrating Management, Technology, and Security.

03 — Core Values
I · P · Q

Innovation · Pioneering · Quality — the three pillars of HSB's sustainable growth, cultivated through six student values: Health, Ethics, Will, Talent, Love, and Duty.
